# School – Mathematics – Study Path {#studienweg}

An educational learning path for lower secondary education (Sekundarstufe I) and upper secondary education leading to the Abitur. This structure does not replace compulsory school curricula.

The following learning paths organise the 52 lessons into four areas: lower secondary (core and enrichment) and upper secondary (Abitur core and advanced/elective). This is educational guidance, not a universally binding curriculum. The thematic blocks are learning paths, not new technical modules. Follow each lesson’s linked prerequisites, particularly M1-L8 before M3-L2. The original modules M1 and M2 retain their stable addresses. The five elective modules M4–M8 may be selected independently once their prerequisites are met.
