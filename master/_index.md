# Course overview – School · Mathematics through upper secondary education {#kurseinstieg}

## Who is this course for? {#S1}

This self-study course progresses from numbers, expressions and geometry to upper secondary calculus, analytic geometry and probability. Basic arithmetic is sufficient to begin. The aim is to construct mathematical models, justify methods and assess results critically. M3 supplements algebraic foundations; M4–M8 are electives. The study path distinguishes lower and upper secondary core and elective routes.
